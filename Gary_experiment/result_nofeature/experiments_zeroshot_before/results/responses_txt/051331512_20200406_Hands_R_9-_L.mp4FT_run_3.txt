### Step 3: Analyze Movement Characteristics
- **Amplitude:** Check if the amplitude (movement range) decreases over time. This can be done by comparing the range of motion in the first half and second half of the taps.
  
  ```python
  # Calculate amplitude for each tap
  amplitudes = []
  for i in range(1, len(data)):
      dy = abs(data['Y_index'].iloc[i] - data['Y_index'].iloc[i-1])
      dx = abs(data['X_index'].iloc[i] - data['X_index'].iloc[i-1])
      amplitude = (dx**2 + dy**2)**0.5
      amplitudes.append(amplitude)
  
  # Plot amplitude over time
  plt.figure(figsize=(12, 6))
  plt.plot(range(len(amplitudes)), amplitudes)
  plt.xlabel('Frame Number')
  plt.ylabel('Amplitude')
  plt.title('Amplitude Over Time')
  plt.show()
  ```

- **Speed:** Measure the time between taps to assess if there is slowing. Inconsistent intervals may indicate hesitations or interruptions.
  
  ```python
  # Calculate time between taps (assuming taps are known)
  tap_frames = [frame for frame in data['frame'][data['tap']]  # Assuming 'tap' column indicates a tap
               if frame is not None]
  intervals = [tap_frames[i+1] - tap_frames[i] for i in range(len(tap_frames)-1)]
  ```

- **Hesitations/Interruptions:** Count the number of hesitations or interruptions in the tapping rhythm. This can be inferred from irregularities in the tap intervals.

### Step 4: Apply MDS-UPDRS Scoring Criteria
Based on the analysis:

1. **Normal (0):** No slowing, consistent rhythm, and full amplitude throughout.
2. **Slight (1):** Minor slowing, 1-2 hesitations, or slight amplitude decrement near the end.
3. **Mild (2):** Moderate slowing, 3-5 hesitations, or amplitude decrement by midpoint.
4. **Moderate (3):** Significant slowing, >5 hesitations, freezes, or early amplitude loss.
5. **Severe (4):** Barely able to perform due to severe slowing or numerous interruptions.

### Step 5: Assign the Score
After analyzing the data:

- If there are fewer than 10 taps, significant slowing, frequent hesitations, or a noticeable decrement in amplitude, assign a higher score.
- If the movement is smooth with consistent rhythm and amplitude, assign a lower score.
