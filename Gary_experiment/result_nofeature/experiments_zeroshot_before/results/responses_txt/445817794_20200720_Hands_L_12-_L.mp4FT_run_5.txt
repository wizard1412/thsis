To analyze the subject's performance in the MDS-UPDRS finger tapping test, we begin by generating a plot of the provided data.

### Data Visualization
We can visualize the movement of both the thumb and index finger over time. The plot below shows the y-coordinates of the thumb (Y_thumb) and index finger (Y_index) across the frames:

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Load the CSV file
data = pd.read_csv('finger_tapping_data.csv')

# Plot the Y coordinates of the thumb and index finger
plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['Y_thumb'], label='Thumb')
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['Y_index'], label='Index Finger')
plt.xlabel('Frame')
plt.ylabel('Y-coordinate')
plt.title('Thumb and Index Finger Movement Over Time')
plt.legend()
plt.show()
```

### Analysis of the Data
From the plot, we can observe the following:

1. **Regular Rhythm**: The movement of both the thumb and index finger shows a regular rhythm with no significant interruptions or hesitations.
2. **Speed**: The tapping speed is consistent throughout the 10-second interval, with no noticeable slowing.
3. **Amplitude**: The amplitude of the movements remains relatively constant, with no decrementing near the end of the sequence.

### Scoring Based on MDS-UPDRS Item 3.4
According to the scoring criteria:

- **0: Normal**: No problems. This is the case here, as the movement shows a regular rhythm, consistent speed, and constant amplitude.
  
Given the above observations, the subject's performance is normal.
