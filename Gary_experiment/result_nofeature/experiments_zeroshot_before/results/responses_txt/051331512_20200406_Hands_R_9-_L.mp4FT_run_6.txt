The evaluation of the CSV data involves analyzing the number of taps, presence of hesitations, and amplitude consistency. Based on the MDS-UPDRS criteria:

- **Score 0**: Normal performance with no issues.
- **Score 1**: Slight difficulty with minimal slowing, one hesitation, and slight amplitude decrease near the end.
- **Score 2**: Noticeable difficulty with moderate slowing, several hesitations, and amplitude decrement mid-way.
- **Score 3**: Significant difficulty with severe slowing, many hesitations, and early amplitude loss.
- **Score 4**: Unable to perform or great difficulty.
