### Step 1: Generate a plot of the data
We start by generating a plot of the thumb and index finger positions over time to visualize the tapping movement. This helps us understand the pattern of taps, including amplitude, rhythm, and any hesitations or interruptions.

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Load the CSV file
data = pd.read_csv('finger_tapping_data.csv')

# Plot the thumb and index finger positions over time
plt.figure(figsize=(12, 6))

plt.subplot(2, 1, 1)
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['X_thumb'], label='Thumb X')
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['Y_thumb'], label='Thumb Y')
plt.title('Thumb Position Over Time')
plt.xlabel('Frame')
plt.ylabel('Position (pixels)')
plt.legend()

plt.subplot(2, 1, 2)
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['X_index'], label='Index X')
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['Y_index'], label='Index Y')
plt.title('Index Finger Position Over Time')
plt.xlabel('Frame')
plt.ylabel('Position (pixels)')
plt.legend()

plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()
```

### Step 3: Evaluate speed, amplitude, hesitations, and halts
We analyze the data to evaluate:
1. **Speed**: The number of taps in 10 seconds indicates tapping speed.
2. **Amplitude**: The range of movement in the Y-direction for both thumb and index finger indicates tap amplitude.
3. **Hesitations/Interruptions**: Irregularities in the tapping pattern indicate hesitations or interruptions.
4. **Halts/Freezes**: Prolonged pauses in tapping indicate halts or freezes.

### Step 4: Assign a score based on MDS-UPDRS criteria
Based on the analysis, we assign a score from 0 to 4 according to the MDS-UPDRS item 3.4 criteria:

- **Score 0**: Normal performance with no hesitations, interruptions, or amplitude decrements.
- **Score 1**: Slight hesitations (1-2 interruptions), slight slowing, or amplitude decrement near the end.
- **Score 2**: Mild hesitations (3-5 interruptions), mild slowing, or amplitude decrement midway.
- **Score 3**: Moderate hesitations (>5 interruptions or freezes), moderate slowing, or early amplitude decrement.
- **Score 4**: Severe performance with inability to complete the task due to significant slowing, interruptions, or decrements.
