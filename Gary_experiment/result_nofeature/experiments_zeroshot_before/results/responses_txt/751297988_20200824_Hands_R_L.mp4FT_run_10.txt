To analyze the finger-tapping data and assign an MDS-UPDRS score, we follow these steps:

1. **Load and Visualize Data**: Plot the thumb and index finger coordinates over time to observe movement patterns.

2. **Calculate Tap Timings**: Use a peak detection method to identify when taps occur by analyzing the distance between fingers.

3. **Analyze Rhythm and Speed**: Compute intervals between taps to check for hesitations or slowing. Regular, short intervals indicate normal performance, while longer or variable intervals suggest issues.

4. **Assess Amplitude Consistency**: Track the maximum distance during each tap to see if amplitudes are consistent, decrease over time, or show significant variation.

5. **Apply MDS-UPDRS Criteria**: Based on the number of hesitations, amplitude changes, and overall speed, assign a score from 0 (normal) to 4 (severe).
