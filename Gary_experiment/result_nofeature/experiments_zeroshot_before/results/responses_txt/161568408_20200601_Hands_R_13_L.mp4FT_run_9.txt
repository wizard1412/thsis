### 4. **Determine the Score Based on MDS-UPDRS Criteria**
Using the results from the analysis, we will assign a score according to the MDS-UPDRS finger tapping criteria:

- **0: Normal**  
  No problems with speed, amplitude, hesitations, halts, or decrementing amplitude.

- **1: Slight**  
  Any of the following:  
  - Regular rhythm is broken with one or two interruptions or hesitations.  
  - Slight slowing.  
  - Amplitude decrements near the end of the 10 taps.

- **2: Mild**  
  Any of the following:  
  - 3 to 5 interruptions during tapping.  
  - Mild slowing.  
  - Amplitude decrements midway in the 10-tap sequence.

- **3: Moderate**  
  Any of the following:  
  - More than 5 interruptions during tapping or at least one longer arrest (freeze) in ongoing movement.  
  - Moderate slowing.  
  - Amplitude decrements starting after the 1st tap.

- **4: Severe**  
  Cannot or can only barely perform the task because of slowing, interruptions, or decrements.
