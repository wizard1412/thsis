### Step 1: Data Visualization
First, I will generate plots of the thumb and index finger positions over time to visualize the tapping movement. The plots will help identify patterns such as amplitude, rhythm, and interruptions.

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Load the data
data = pd.read_csv('finger_tapping.csv')

# Plot the Y coordinates of the thumb and index finger over time
plt.figure(figsize=(12, 6))
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['Y_thumb'], label='Thumb (Y)')
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['Y_index'], label='Index Finger (Y)')
plt.xlabel('Frame')
plt.ylabel('Position (pixels)')
plt.title('Vertical Movement of Thumb and Index Finger Over Time')
plt.legend()
plt.show()

# Plot the X coordinates of the thumb and index finger over time
plt.figure(figsize=(12, 6))
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['X_thumb'], label='Thumb (X)')
plt.plot(data['frame'], data['X_index'], label='Index Finger (X)')
plt.xlabel('Frame')
plt.ylabel('Position (pixels)')
plt.title('Horizontal Movement of Thumb and Index Finger Over Time')
plt.legend()
plt.show()
```
