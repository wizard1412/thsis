The data provided below consists of kinematic features extracted from a subject performing the MDS-UPDRS Item 3.4 Finger Tapping test. The subject was instructed to tap the index finger on the thumb 10 times as quickly and as largely as possible.

Your task is to score the subject according to MDS-UPDRS Item 3.4 using a step-by-step approach: evaluate each of the three scoring dimensions separately, then determine the final score.

MDS-UPDRS Item 3.4 scoring criteria:
  0: Normal. No problems.
  1: Slight. Any of the following: a) 1-2 interruptions or hesitations; b) slight slowing; c) amplitude decrements near the END of the 10 taps.
  2: Mild.   Any of the following: a) 3-5 interruptions; b) mild slowing; c) amplitude decrements MIDWAY in the sequence.
  3: Moderate. Any of the following: a) >5 interruptions or at least one freeze; b) moderate slowing; c) amplitude decrements starting after the 1ST tap.
  4: Severe. Cannot or can barely perform the task.

IMPORTANT: Each dimension is scored independently. The final score equals the WORST (highest) of the three dimension scores — not an average.
